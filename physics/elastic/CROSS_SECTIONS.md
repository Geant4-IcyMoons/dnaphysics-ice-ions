# Elastic cross sections

The [carbon NLH pair DCS catalogue](../cross_sections/elastic/carbon_nlh_pair/)
contains a qualified binary cross-section index and an archive of its 962
carbon–H and carbon–O maps. See the [catalogue guide](../cross_sections/README.md)
for verified extraction and the [production protocol](hard_collisions/README.md)
for the limits of these pair products.
