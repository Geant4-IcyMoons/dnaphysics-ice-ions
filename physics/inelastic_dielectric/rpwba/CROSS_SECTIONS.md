# RPWBA tables

Completed H⁺ and H⁰ RPWBA tables are in
[physics/cross_sections/inelastic_dielectric/rpwba](../../cross_sections/inelastic_dielectric/rpwba/).
Both ice phases and both polarization settings are present. H⁰ polarization-on
products are diagnostic only; their flags accompany the compressed DAT files.
