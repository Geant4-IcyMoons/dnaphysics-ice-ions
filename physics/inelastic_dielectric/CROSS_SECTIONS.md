# Generated dielectric cross sections

Completed hydrogen tables are catalogued in
[physics/cross_sections/inelastic_dielectric](../cross_sections/inelastic_dielectric/).
See the [catalogue guide](../cross_sections/README.md) to restore the original
DAT files and inspect completion provenance. PWBA and RPWBA have separate
subdirectories. The active helium campaign still writes to its production
output until each case is accepted for the catalogue.
