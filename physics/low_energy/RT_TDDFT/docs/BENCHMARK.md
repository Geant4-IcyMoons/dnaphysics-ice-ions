# Independent H+--H2O capture calculation

## Scope and status

`benchmark.py` runs one 1 keV H+ encounter with one isolated water molecule.
It prepares neutral-water orbitals, propagates electrons and all four nuclei
with Ehrenfest dynamics, transfers the outgoing orbitals to an inertial frame
moving with the proton, and propagates further before counting electrons.
The solver is Octopus 16.4; its source does not need modification.

The complete chain has completed at full resolution. Projectile-frame
continuation through 600 au passes the recorded stationarity criterion.
**Numerical convergence and comparison with published capture curves have not
been established.** Execution success and late-time stationarity are separate
checks; neither establishes physical accuracy. No excitation or exclusive
ionization probabilities, ice-phase results, or transport cross sections are
produced by this calculation.

## Run

From the repository root, with NumPy and SciPy installed and Octopus on PATH:

```bash
python -m physics.low_energy.RT_TDDFT.benchmark \
  --output physics/low_energy/RT_TDDFT/runs/hplus_water_1keV_b2
```

`--executable /absolute/path/to/octopus` selects a particular installation.
The runner copies H.psf and O.psf from its installed PSF directory and records
their SHA-256 hashes. `--pseudopotentials` can select another directory explicitly.
Each run requires a new output directory and records solver version, executable
and implementation hashes, input hashes, logs, and output provenance.

For ChemFarm, submit the supplied PBS script from the repository root:

```bash
qsub -v RT_CASE_PATH=/gpfs01/work/yoffegid/hplus_water_1keV_b2 \
  physics/low_energy/RT_TDDFT/jobs/hplus_water.pbs
```

It requests one node with 12 MPI ranks, 64 GB and eight hours in the medium
queue, activates the existing Linux Octopus installation, and keeps OpenMP and
BLAS at one thread per rank. Adjust scheduler resources with `qsub -l` if needed.
The script starts one encounter, not 12 independent collisions. Independent
impact parameters can use separate jobs. `RT_EXECUTION_CHECK=1` selects the
reduced-resolution execution check; override queue, memory and walltime for it.

The runner also accepts a generic `--launcher 'mpirun -np 12'` (or a scheduler's
launcher) inside an existing allocation. It passes arguments without shell
evaluation. On the local Mac, Octopus has OpenMP but no MPI.

`--prepare-only` writes the initial ground-state and collision inputs and the
settings without executing them. The final handoff input must be generated by
the runner from measured positions, velocities and orbitals; it cannot be
specified correctly before the collision. `--help` lists numerical overrides.
The authoritative defaults are `benchmark.Settings`.

## Adopted model and departures from the reference

The comparison target is [Hong et al., PRA 93, 062706 (2016)](
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.93.062706), particularly Sections II--III and
Figure 2 at 1 keV. Its benchmark does not establish accuracy at 100 eV.

- Geometry: the experimental Cartesian water coordinates in the
  [NIST CCCBDB](https://cccbdb.nist.gov/expgeom2x.asp?casno=7732185), translated
  to put O at the origin and rotated into xy with the bisector along +x.
  Coordinates are stored explicitly in every receipt. This named orientation
  has not been mapped to a Hong Figure 1 label; do not silently equate it to a--f.
  The initial nuclear velocities of water are zero. These experimental
  coordinates are not asserted to be an LDA equilibrium geometry.
- Electrons: eight occupied spin orbitals, four per spin; oxygen 1s is frozen.
  Neutral H2O is calculated without the projectile. H is added only in TD with
  total charge +1, retaining the neutral-target orbitals on the identical mesh.
- Electronic approximation: explicitly `lda_x + lda_c_pz`, with the bundled
  Troullier--Martins PSF potentials. These are our recorded choices; the exact
  identities used by Hong are not established. The proton uses the H potential
  with no initial bound electron. Its core regularization requires a convergence
  and reference check, particularly for close encounters.
- Propagation: ETRS, a nine-point stencil per Cartesian direction, 0.33 bohr
  spacing, and a 0.025 atomic-unit timestep. All nuclei move under Ehrenfest
  forces; there is no prescribed-velocity constraint or added NLH force.
- Collision box: a larger rectangular lab-frame box retains the outgoing
  projectile and its electrons before handoff. This deliberately differs from
  the reported spherical calculation. Its transverse half-lengths are about
  35 bohr and longitudinal half-length about 65 bohr at the defaults. This is
  a cluster calculation: even one eight-orbital complex128 array is of order
  2 GiB; solver work arrays, densities, output, and restarts add substantially.
- The projectile begins at (-15,b,0), with b=2 bohr by default. Flight duration
  aims for x=45 bohr at the initial speed; handoff requires a **measured** minimum
  distance of 40 bohr from the water nuclei and enough box clearance for the
  entire destination sphere. A failure asks for a longer flight or larger box,
  rather than translating an incompletely separated collision.
- The projectile-frame sphere has radius 25 bohr and a sine-squared imaginary
  absorber over 20--25 bohr with magnitude 2 Ha. Initial clearing duration is
  200 atomic units, about 4.84 fs, with five samples. These are starting settings,
  not a demonstrated sufficient clearing time. The largest probability change
  over the final three samples must be <=0.001 for the stationarity flag.

## Orbital handoff

At the measured handoff time, R and v are the outgoing proton position and
velocity. The code applies, in atomic units,

```text
r' = r - R
V_nucleus' = V_nucleus - v
psi'(r') = exp(-i v . r') psi(r' + R)
```

An irrelevant common global phase is omitted. Cubic Cartesian interpolation
samples the lab-frame orbitals on the destination sphere. Integer grid shifts
are sampled exactly. Cropping and interpolation are not unitary; lost norm is
recorded, never restored by normalization. All eight original orbital labels
are retained. Octopus reads these native complex .obf files through
`UserDefinedStates` with `normalize_no` and `OnlyUserDefinedInitialStates=yes`.
Relative paths avoid Octopus's finite-length orbital filename buffer.

A one-step artificial calculation exports the exact destination mesh. Its
orbitals have no physical meaning and are deleted after mesh extraction.
The subsequent physical calculation imports the transformed collision orbitals.
Their overlaps and integrated spin densities are checked at its initial step,
so an accidental occupation change or normalization cannot pass unnoticed.

The new frame is inertial. All nuclei continue Ehrenfest motion; the proton is
not artificially pinned. Its displacement from the sphere center must stay
below the recorded limit (default 1 bohr). The counting region is the whole
fixed sphere, including its absorber; water nuclei must remain outside it.

Cropping removes the remote target's electronic density while its nuclei remain
in the potential. This changes the residual electrostatic field and must be
controlled by separation convergence. A velocity boost of nonlocal potentials
is not implemented; its significance must be assessed before extending this
handoff to ions with bound electrons or quantum regions containing nonlocal
projectors. This H+ path must not be reused blindly for C/O/S.

## Outputs and validation

`result.json` records execution and handoff checks. `capture.json` contains
P(0)...P(8), P(1), mean electron count, and the P(n>2) contamination diagnostic
at each clearing sample. Counting uses the existing full-overlap determinant
implementation in `capture.py`. P(1) is inclusive over residual-target states;
it does not prove an intact H2O+ product. The runner does not convert an
unconverged regional population into a validated neutral-hydrogen yield.

The existing collector accepts the runner's analyses once stationarity passes:

```bash
python -m physics.low_energy.RT_TDDFT.capture_curve \
  physics/low_energy/RT_TDDFT/runs/hplus_water_1keV_b2/capture.json \
  --output capture_curve.csv
```

This supplies 2*pi*b*P(1), not an integrated cross section. A published comparison
still requires matching geometry/orientation and numerical reference data.

Before adopting capture values, vary spacing, timestep, box and absorber,
initial and final separation, interpolation resolution, and clearing duration.
Check an unperturbed water control and isolated moving-H control as well.
A passing time-stationarity flag is not a substitute for those tests.
Then sample impact parameters and orientations. Ice extensions should use the
accepted models through the existing structure loader after the molecular
calculation is numerically assessed.

### Reproduce the execution test

```bash
OCTOPUS_TEST_EXECUTABLE=/absolute/path/to/octopus \
python -m pytest tests/test_rt_tddft.py tests/test_rt_tddft_hplus.py \
  tests/test_rt_tddft_capture.py tests/test_rt_tddft_benchmark.py -q
```

Without that environment variable, the real-solver test is explicitly skipped.
It uses 0.8 bohr spacing, 0.1 atomic-unit steps, shorter separations and clearing,
and a smaller sphere. It checks the full GS -> collision -> frame transformation
-> clearing -> probability path, not benchmark accuracy. Analytical tests also
check a translated moving Gaussian, the sign of its velocity phase, exact grid
translations, retained norm loss, and native complex-file round trips.

The local Octopus 16.4 check on 2026-09-15 passed all 34 tests. Initial orbital
orthogonality error was 1.02e-13; the neutral-target restart and transformed-orbital
import showed zero amplitude and overlap differences, respectively. The short
clearing test changed the probability distribution by 0.00226, exceeding its
0.001 stationarity tolerance. No accepted capture probability was obtained.
Temporary solver files from this execution check were removed; the test above
recreates the calculation.

The same case completed on ChemFarm with 12 MPI ranks (PBS job 245982.pbs02,
exit status zero). The largest difference from the local probability arrays
was below 1e-10. The compact [verification receipt](../checks/hplus_capture/verification.json)
records settings, software hashes and checks. Both runs fail the deliberately
short clearing-time stationarity test; agreement establishes execution
consistency, not physical accuracy.

The initial full-resolution attempt (245981.pbs02) was killed by signal 9 during
METIS partitioning, before SCF. Memory pressure is suspected but not confirmed
by the PBS record. Its [compressed log](../checks/hplus_capture/metis_startup_failure.log.gz)
is retained. The adopted Hilbert partitioner avoids constructing the global
partitioning graph; it changes process ownership of grid points, not the physics
settings. Both local and MPI checks were repeated successfully after this change.

The full-resolution retry (245983.pbs02) passed partitioning and entered SCF.
It was cancelled at the user's request on 2026-09-15 (PBS exit status 143).
All files deployed or generated for this benchmark attempt were removed from

### ChemFarm full-resolution result and clearing continuation

Job 246063.pbs02 completed the full-resolution calculation on 2026-09-16
with 256 CPUs (32 MPI ranks, eight OpenMP threads each) and 128 GB RAM.
All solver stages returned zero. At 200 au of projectile-frame clearing,
P(1) = 0.3359338585. The maximum probability range over the last three
samples was 0.001234596, exceeding the unchanged 0.001 stationarity tolerance.
This value is therefore not an accepted capture yield.

`continue_clearing.py` extends the native TD restart, including the complete
nuclear trajectory required by Octopus to restore positions and velocities.
It checks checkpoint probabilities against the preceding analysis and keeps
the original collision and clearing results unchanged. Each completed stage
writes its analysis atomically. An interrupted stage can be retried from its
intact predecessor; this may repeat at most one original clearing duration.
Run this continuation on a compute node with the same solver installation:

```bash
python -m physics.low_energy.RT_TDDFT.continue_clearing \
  --case /absolute/path/to/case --executable /absolute/path/to/octopus \
  --launcher 'mpirun -np 32' --end-time 600
```

For the completed case, this adds two 200-au stages, retaining samples every
50 au and the original stationarity criterion. Results appear separately in
`case/clearing_0016000/capture.json` and `case/clearing_0024000/capture.json`.
PBS job 247653.pbs02 was submitted on 2026-09-17 with the same 256-CPU,
128-GB allocation. It completed with exit status zero. The probability range was 0.000388410
at 400 au and 0.000302942 at 600 au; both pass the 0.001 tolerance.
The final P(1) is 0.335077333. See the [benchmark figure](../benchmarks/hplus_water_1kev/README.md).
Passing stationarity alone does not establish numerical convergence.

The superseded 64-GB attempt's ground-state output and restart were removed
after the successful run completed. Its compact failure diagnostics and
submission provenance remain; the successful run's scientific outputs and
checkpoints are retained.
