# Ice-structure benchmarks

Structure benchmarks compare density, lattice constants, O--O radial
distribution functions, structure factors and crystalline-order observables
with phase- and temperature-matched experimental or peer-reviewed references.
Amorphous and ice-Ih results are never interchanged, and agreement in density
alone is insufficient.

The model and adapted melt--quench provenance are recorded in
`../../../nep_mbpol/PROVENANCE.json` and `../../../nep_mbpol/README.md`.

The experimental-data-constrained alternative for 80 K LDA is implemented in
`../../../ice_structures/epsr_lda80k/`. Its source archive preserves the three
isotope-resolved neutron datasets and the corresponding EPSR fits, so their
comparison is retained directly instead of inferring LDA from density alone.
